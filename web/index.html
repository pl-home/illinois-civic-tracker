Sources & limits

Dates and evidence stay visible.

{{SOURCE_COUNT}} calendar/committee source receipts · {{ERROR_COUNT}} calendar collection errors. Only posted meetings matching a member’s committee assignments are included. A committee assignment is not proof of attendance. Published feeds may not cover the entire upcoming month.

Bill status changes are labeled separately from actions by a member. Introduction dates never substitute for an unknown cosponsorship join date. Senate nominations and procedural votes retain the recorded motion. Illinois floor and committee votes remain distinct.
